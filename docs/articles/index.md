# Articles

### Charts in R

- [Get
  started](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemstockindices/articles/golemstockindices.md):

  Download the prices of the major stock market indices in R and draw
  interactive candlestick charts, compare indices in one currency and
  see their monthly returns.

- [Volatility, drawdowns and
  seasonality](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemstockindices/articles/analyses.md):

  The volatility, drawdowns and seasonality of a stock market index in
  R, with a GARCH forecast of its volatility.

- [The market
  overview](https://sedzinfo.github.io/golemstockindices/articles/market.md):

  Where every major stock market index stands against its last close,
  the start of the year, a year ago and its all-time high, and which
  exchanges are open, in R.
